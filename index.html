The important question

Why are seals so lovable?

I like fat seals because they look like happy little blobs in the ocean. They have round bodies, funny faces, and a talent for looking completely unbothered. It's hard not to smile when you see one relaxing on a beach or popping its head out of the water.

🫧

Peak blob energy

On land, seals can look like one big squishy shape. Their clumsy-looking movements make them even funnier.

😌

🌊

Ocean athletes

More than just cute faces

Some seal facts

Seals are marine mammals in a group called pinnipeds. Their bodies are adapted for swimming, and their layer of blubber helps keep them warm in cold water.

  • Built for swimming: Their streamlined bodies help them move through the water.
  • Warm and cozy: Blubber provides insulation and stores energy.
  • Whisker power: Their sensitive whiskers can help detect movement in the water.
  • Not all the same: There are many seal species, with different sizes, colors, and habitats.

Meet the seal squad

Different seals, same cute energy

🦭

Harbor seal

Often seen resting on beaches and rocky shores. Harbor seals have spotted coats and round heads.

❄️

Harp seal

Known for the dark, harp-shaped marking on the backs of many adults. They live in the North Atlantic and Arctic.

🌊

Elephant seal

These giants are famous for their huge size. Adult males have a distinctive enlarged nose.

Seal species have different needs and habitats, but every one deserves respect and space in the wild.

Final seal of approval

Protect the blobs!

Seals are wild animals, not pets. We can appreciate them by keeping a respectful distance, never feeding them, and supporting healthy oceans.
